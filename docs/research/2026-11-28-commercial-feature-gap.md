# Commercial feature gap: what expensive tools do that PCPorter does not (2026-11-28)

30 research agents (10 per category) studied commercial forensic suites, data recovery tools and PC backup/migration products, then one agent per category merged and de-duplicated the findings (651 raw items / 512 raw items / 560 raw items -> 724 distinct features). A final agent ranked the top 20 across all three. Claims come from vendor pages, manuals and reviews found by the agents; check a source before building on it. Items that break PCPorter rules (no cracking or decryption bypass, no writes to the scanned PC, no LSA/SAM secrets, no covert or law-enforcement-only features) are kept in the list and marked **rule conflict: not building**.

Status: **Missing** = not in PCPorter; **Partial** = some of it exists; **Designed, not built** = in the design plan only. Effort for one developer: S small, M medium, L large, XL very large.

### Honorable mentions

- Signature carving of photos, documents and videos from free space for formatted or RAW drives (13+ products, L); it is the natural next step after MFT undelete.
- Bootable WinPE rescue USB that runs PCPorter's existing CLI mode on a PC that will not start, so a soldered SSD does not have to be removed (16+ products across all three categories, L).
- Boot the old PC's image as a Hyper-V VM on a differencing disk, so old apps can still be opened after the clean rebuild (Macrium viBoot, Veeam Agent, Forensic Explorer Live Boot, L).
- Lost or deleted partition search with read-only virtual browsing that never rewrites the partition table (TestDisk, R-Studio, DMDE, UFS Explorer, L).
- Unified super-timeline with a relative time-anchor filter and an hour-of-day activity heatmap, tying all the designed activity sources together (Plaso, Magnet Axiom, X-Ways, FTK; designed, L).
- USB device and Wi-Fi/network connection history, showing which drives were plugged in and which networks to rejoin on the new PC (Oxygen, Cellebrite, OSForensics, FTK, KAPE; USB designed, M).
- Carry-over of mapped network drives, network printers, user-installed fonts and Wi-Fi profiles from the live PC (USMT, PCmover, ForensiT Transwiz, Windows Backup; S to M).
- Outlook PST/OST viewer with export to EML/MBOX, including orphaned OSTs from a dead PC's profile (Stellar, Kernel and most forensic suites, L).
- NSRL known-good filtering plus exact-hash duplicate stacking, to shrink both review lists and backup size (11 forensic suites and ReclaiMe Pro, M).
- A restore point on the new PC before anything is written, plus one-click undo of the whole migration (EaseUS PCTrans, PCmover, Liquidware ProfileUnity, Zinstall; designed, M to L).

Ranking method: I merged duplicates across the three gap lists first. Imaging, BitLocker unlock, shadow copies, MFT undelete, the Recycle Bin and image-as-source each appear in two or three categories, so each became one entry under a single category, and its seen_in list counts products from all three. I scored each candidate for value to the four purposes (clean move, what happened, health and security, data recovery) and divided by effort (S=1, M=2, L=3, XL=4). Ties went to features that build on existing or designed code and that appear in 3 or more expensive products. Several small S-effort items were bundled into one coherent feature each: the hijack checks, the don't-lose-it finder, and remote access with camera and microphone use. I dropped every conflicts_with_rules=true item and scoped borderline ones to stay read-only: SMART self-tests are excluded, the Defender scan runs with remediation off, shadow copies are opened by device path with no links created, BitLocker opens only with the owner's own key, vaults and wallets are only located, Defender quarantine shows metadata only, and VirusTotal lookups are opt-in and send hashes only. Encrypted backup is optional so the owner's choice of a plain-text Passwords.html stays the default. The balance is 7 forensic, 7 recovery and 6 backup/migration picks. Three pick pairs depend on each other: rank 2 should come before 20 and 19, rank 20 feeds 9, and ranks 16, 18 and 19 should share the designed MFT and timeline parsers.
